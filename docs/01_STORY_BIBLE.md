# SHADOW PROTOCOL: NASSER 07 — Story Bible

Version 1.0 · Original work · All persons and operations fictional.

---

## 1. Logline

An Egyptian special-operations commander hunting a hostage crisis in the present discovers
that the conspiracy he is unraveling was written down by his own grandfather — an officer who
crossed the Suez Canal in 1973 and chose to hide one page of the truth instead of burning it.

---

## 2. Themes

- **Inheritance over bloodline.** Nasser does not inherit heroism; he inherits a *question*.
- **The cost of the quiet choice.** Taher's small act of concealment saves lives in 1973 and
  kills people in the present. The game never lets the player call it simply "right".
- **The institution as a person.** Egyptian intelligence is portrayed as a family of committed
  professionals corrupted at the top, never as a monolith. Loyalty to the country, not the office.
- **The archive always wins.** Paper outlives soldiers. Whoever controls the ledger controls
  the story of the war.

**Tone rules**
- No triumphalism about any real army; heroism is shown at squad scale, in dust and blood.
- The enemy is always specific: soldiers, brokers, officers, not nationalities.
- Arabic is the emotional language of the game. When characters are alone, they speak Egyptian
  Arabic. Radio traffic is bilingual.

## 3. The two generations

### Generation One — العقيد طاهر عبد الحكيم · Colonel TAHER ABDEL-HAKIM
**Callsign:** NISR-3 ("Eagle") · **Born:** 1931, Mansoura · **Unit:** Egyptian Commando
(Sa'ka) battalion, attached to Second Army for the canal crossing.

- Career officer, 22 years of service, wounded at Port Said in 1956.
- Commands a 40-man commando detachment. Known for refusing to spend men on paperwork.
- Quiet, precise, superstitious about one thing: he keeps his men's names written on the
  inside of his helmet liner.
- Believes the army is the country's spine, and that staff officers are its arthritis.
- **The wound:** in 1967 he lost a brother in the Sinai withdrawal and was ordered to leave
  wounded men behind. He has never obeyed an order to abandon a soldier since.

### Generation Two — ناصر · NASSER 07
**Full name:** Major Nasser Abdel-Hakim · **Callsign:** NASSER-07 ·
**Unit:** Egyptian General Intelligence Directorate, Special Operations Cell "K-9"

- Age 36. Father was a navy engineer; mother is a Cairo University linguist. Taher's grandson.
- Grew up with Taher's helmet on a shelf and one unanswered question about it.
- Analytical by temperament, decisive by training. Speaks Egyptian Arabic, English, Russian,
  passable Korean.
- Known in the Directorate for one habit: he reads the footnotes of every file he is handed.
- **The number 07** is not a rank — it is the archive box number of his grandfather's
  sealed file. He was assigned the callsign by a clerk who never knew the coincidence was
  deliberate.
- **The wound:** he believes institutions can be repaired from inside. The campaign tests this.

## 5. The conspiracy — OPERATION AMUN / THE BLACK LEDGER

**1973.** During the October War, an Egyptian logistics officer diverts a convoy of
artillery ammunition and medical stores meant for the crossing at Qantara. The convoy is
sold across the lines to a broker network that will become **THE SCALE** (Arabic: *Al-Mizan*).

The arrangement is recorded in a ledger — *the Black Ledger* — and duplicated in a single
carbon copy filed as **ARCHIVE BOX 07, OPERATION AMUN** inside a military intelligence
annex.

Taher's detachment is sent to recover the convoy on 9 October 1973. He finds the ledger,
not the ammunition. He tears out the page naming the officers involved — including a young
staff captain, **Ashraf Zidan** — and hides it inside his helmet liner. He reports the convoy
destroyed.

He keeps the page for 41 years. He never uses it.

**Present day.** *Shadow Protocol* is the standing, unsigned directive that the network uses
to erase the remaining trace of Box 07: witnesses are removed, archives are "corrected",
and the file is quietly reclassified every eleven years. The network has become a
transnational intelligence brokerage selling access rather than weapons.

**The modern trigger:** a North Korean facility is holding Egyptian engineers who were
recovering a crashed drone that carried a copy of Box 07's scan. Nasser is sent to rescue
"hostages". He is actually being sent to destroy evidence — and he does not know it.

**The confirmation:** the page in Taher's helmet matches a scan Nasser finds in Port Komdok.

## 7. Chapter climaxes (one line each)

1. **The Crossing** — Taher holds the canal bridgehead, then finds a ledger where ammunition should be.
2. **The Silent Mission** — Nasser rescues the hostages on the roof of Port Komdok and finds his own grandfather's handwriting in a scanned file.
3. **The Black File** — Hoda proves the file was *edited* in 1974, 1985, 1996, 2007 and 2018 — every eleven years, by the same signature block.
4. **City of Shadows** — Nasser tracks KONSUL through Vienna's Canal District and learns the network's name: *The Scale*.
5. **Betrayal** — the team is ambushed at the safehouse; Rami dies; the player chooses whom to trust — and lives with it.
6. **Shadow Protocol** — beneath the Western Desert, the Curator is revealed as Taher's radio operator, and the true purpose of Shadow Protocol is spoken aloud.
7. **The Final Legacy** — Meridian Tower falls; Nasser decides what to do with the page.

## 8. Endings

Four tracked flags:
`TRUST_ADEL`, `SAVED_MARIAM`, `SPARED_CURATOR`, `PUBLISHED_LEDGER`.

| Ending | Requirements | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **CLEAN HANDS** | TRUST_ADEL + SAVED_MARIAM + PUBLISHED_LEDGER | The ledger is published through a civilian archive. Zidan is arrested. Hoda and Adel testify. Nasser resigns his commission and keeps the helmet. Bittersweet, institutional. |
| **THE LEDGER** | !PUBLISHED_LEDGER + SPARED_CURATOR | Nasser keeps the ledger and uses it as leverage inside the Directorate. The network is decapitated, not dismantled. Cold, ambiguous, sequel hook. |
| **INHERITANCE** | !TRUST_ADEL or team deaths ≥ 2, any publication state | Nasser burns the page beside Taher's grave. The conspiracy survives underground; Nasser is reassigned to a unit that does not exist. Darkest ending, strongest sequel hook. |

A fourth, hidden outcome — **THE QUIET CHOICE** — triggers if the player found all 21
intelligence documents and kept Adel alive: an epilogue in 1973 showing Taher deciding
*not* to burn the page. Unlocks New Game+ with the 1973 winter uniform.

## 9. Historical accuracy charter

- **Portrayed accurately:** Egyptian 1973 uniforms (sand-drab fatigues, webbing, steel helmets),
  AKM/AK-47, RPD, RPG-7, Port Said submachine gun, B-10 recoilless rifles, T-55/T-62 tanks,
  BTR-50/OT-62 APCs, Mi-8 helicopters, bridging equipment, Egyptian and Israeli radio procedure,
  the Bar Lev Line's sand berms and strongpoints, the 6 October 14:00 H-hour, the 9 October
  Israeli counterattacks.
- **Explicitly fictional:** Operation Amun, Box 07, the Black Ledger, all named characters,
  the diversion of the Qantara convoy, and every event after 1973.
- **Never portrayed:** real historical figures in fabricated criminal acts; atrocity as
  spectacle; any claim that this fiction describes real events.
- An in-game disclaimer appears on first launch and in the Credits screen.

---

## 10. Names & conventions

- Egyptian characters use Egyptian colloquial Arabic in intimate scenes; Modern Standard
  Arabic for official radio and documents. Israeli and Korean characters speak their own
  languages with subtitles; English is the "operations" language.
- Squad callsigns are English nouns (FANG, JACKAL, MOUSE, GLASS) because Egyptian special
  operations teams commonly use such brevity codes; the Arabic banter never uses them.
- The number **07** appears on every archive cover in the campaign — a visual motif.
